How do you find the lowest price?

We check each partner seller's listed price every 15 minutes and tag the cheapest offer. The full list stays visible so you can pick faster delivery if you prefer.

Who ships my order?

The seller you choose ships it. Pricedekho handles payment and tracking, so you deal with one place for every order.

Can I pay cash on delivery?

Yes, on most products. Cards and mobile wallets are also accepted at checkout.

What if the product is faulty?

Request a return within 7 days from your orders page. We arrange pickup and refund once the seller confirms receipt.

Are warranties official?

Each listing states its warranty type. Products marked Official warranty are covered by the brand's service centres.

Do prices change after I add to cart?

The price is held for 30 minutes once you start checkout. If it drops in that time, you get the lower price.
